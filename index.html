      How it works

      Sensor

      Above the water in each tank or compartment.

      Controller

      Works out level and litres; keeps months of history.

      Level bar

      Colour LED bar where people pass.

      Phones

      Live in the app or a browser.

      Features

      • Live level and litres for every tank and compartment
      • Alerts for low water, sensor faults and filling
      • Night leak check flags a level that drops unused
      • Motor run time and every fill, per day
      • Water used per day and the busiest hour
      • Reports: PDF, data export, WhatsApp sharing
      • Self-healing with a built-in sensor test
      • Updates over Wi-Fi, no site visit needed
      • Your data stays with you, no cloud account
