          How it works

          Sensor

          Above the water in each tank or compartment.

          Controller

          Works out level and litres; keeps months of history.

          Level bar

          Colour LED bar where people pass.

          Phones

          Live in the app or a browser.

          Features

          • Live level and litres for every tank and compartment
          • Alerts for low water, sensor faults and filling
          • Night leak check flags a level that drops unused
          • Motor runs from when to when: filling 06:00 → 06:40, then any time at 100 %
          • Time at 100 %: spots a motor left running after the tank is full (possible overflow)
          • Water used per day and the busiest hour
          • Reports: PDF, data export, WhatsApp sharing
          • Self-healing with a built-in sensor test
          • Updates over Wi-Fi, no site visit needed
          • Your data stays with you, no cloud account
          • Phone app: possible-overflow and low-water alerts, and offline copies to show at meetings without Wi-Fi
          • Export data (CSV) for spreadsheets, with every level, motor run and time at 100 %
          • Water & electricity wastage report: weekly or monthly, litres and units (₹) wasted by overflow, compared with the period before
          • One motor run, not many short fills: pauses while water is drawn during filling count as one run
          • Downloaded reports in the phone app: every PDF and CSV listed by day, to open, share on WhatsApp or delete
